How it works

Four steps, agreed before work begins.

The process is intentionally simple. You always know what is being researched, what evidence will be used, and what you will receive.

01

Share your brief

Tell us about your business, target market, research question, and any competitors you already know. We confirm scope before work begins.

02

Research competitors

We collect relevant public evidence—product pages, listed prices, promotions, positioning, and public review themes—and record source URLs and access dates.

03

Analyze and review

AI assists with organizing and drafting the analysis. A human checks every factual claim against the recorded sources before delivery.

04

Receive your report

You get a source-linked PDF with findings, five opportunities, limitations, and a prioritized action plan. Delivery timing is agreed in your brief.

About the studio

AI-assisted research. Human-checked delivery.

Market Insight Studio is an independent, early-stage research service for small businesses. We use AI to help organize public information and draft analysis, but we do not send AI output straight to clients.

Every factual claim is checked against the recorded source by a human before a report is delivered. The report separates what the evidence says from our interpretation, and it states clearly when information is missing.

We do not access private competitor data, login-gated pages, or information that is not publicly available. We also do not promise business outcomes: the report is research, not a guarantee.

Our research principles

  • Publicly sourced evidence
  • Transparent source references
  • A clear distinction between facts and interpretations
  • Honest reporting of missing information and limitations
  • No guaranteed business outcomes
  • Respect for privacy and website terms

FAQ

Questions worth asking before you commission research.

What does the report include?

A three-competitor snapshot covering public pricing and promotions, positioning and presentation, public review themes where enough evidence exists, five opportunities to investigate, a prioritized action plan, source references, and a limitations section. It is delivered as a PDF.

Which businesses can use this service?

Small e-commerce and consumer-product businesses—clothing, skincare, home accessories, and similar. It works best when the competitors have public product pages and at least some publicly visible customer feedback.

How are competitors selected?

You can name them, or we can discuss a shortlist based on your market and research question. The final three competitors are agreed before research begins.

Are the findings verified?

AI assists with analysis and drafting. A human checks factual claims against the recorded public sources before delivery. The report distinguishes evidence from interpretation and records source URLs and access dates.

What if public information is unavailable?

We report the gap rather than fill it with guesses. If a competitor has too little public evidence, we may suggest a substitute or adjust the scope with you before work continues.

Do you guarantee increased sales?

No. The report is research, not a promise of results. It is designed to help you make better-informed decisions and identify opportunities worth testing.

How does the client request a report?

Use the enquiry section below to prepare a brief, then send it by email. If an online form has been configured, you can use that instead. We confirm scope and price before work begins.

What payment methods are available?

Payment arrangements are confirmed directly before the project starts. No payment is collected through this website.
